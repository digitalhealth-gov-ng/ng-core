ActorDefinition: NG Registry Service

Official URL: https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/ActorDefinition/NgCoreRegistryService Version: 0.0.0
Draft as of 2026-08-18 Computable Name: NgCoreRegistryService
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113884.3.9944.1.12.2

A registry service that maintains or provides access to authoritative information about governed health-system entities.

Defines the role responsible for trusted registration, identification, lookup, matching, verification, and retrieval of entities shared across health programs, facilities, and interoperable systems.

Actor: NgCoreRegistryService Nigeria Core Registry ServiceType: system

Role

The Nigeria Core Registry Service provides authoritative or governed information about entities such as:

  • patients or clients;
  • practitioners and health workers;
  • organisations and health facilities;
  • locations and healthcare services;
  • pharmacies and laboratories;
  • payers, products, devices, or other governed entities.

A Registry Service may operate nationally, subnationally, institutionally, or within a federated registry architecture.

Responsibilities

A Registry Service may:

  • register entities using applicable Nigeria Core profiles;
  • maintain and verify governed identifiers;
  • provide authorised lookup and search;
  • support matching, duplicate detection, linking, and reconciliation;
  • manage updates and status changes;
  • preserve appropriate provenance and history; and
  • publish supported capabilities.

Typical resource mappings include:

Practitioner role PractitionerRole

Service directory HealthcareService

Conformance expectations

A Registry Service SHALL:

  • use applicable Nigeria Core profiles;
  • use nationally governed identifiers where defined;
  • preserve identifier systems, namespaces, and issuing authorities;
  • comply with applicable profile and terminology constraints;
  • return FHIR-conformant responses; and
  • provide an accurate CapabilityStatement when exposing a FHIR endpoint.

It SHALL NOT issue or redefine a nationally governed identifier without the authority to do so.

Identity and stewardship

Where matching is supported, sufficient identifiers and demographic or other governed attributes SHOULD be used to minimise false matches and duplicate registrations. Algorithmic matching SHOULD be governed, tested, and auditable.

Registry governance SHOULD define the authoritative source, identifier lifecycle, update rules, data-quality controls, access policies, stewardship, and duplicate/conflict resolution.

Access to registry information SHALL follow applicable security and privacy requirements according to the sensitivity of the registry domain.

Relationships

The Registry Service commonly interacts with the Nigeria Core Requestor, Responder, Terminology Service, and Conformance and Sandbox Service.

A Registry Service may itself act as a Requestor when consulting another authoritative service.
