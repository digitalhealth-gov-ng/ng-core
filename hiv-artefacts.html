This page provides a consolidated catalogue of the FHIR artefacts developed for the Nigeria HIV programme. It brings together foundational and clinical profiles, structured programme forms, completed form examples, programme registers and worksheets, aggregate reporting artefacts, terminology resources, mappings to established clinical terminologies, and exchange documents. Links are relative to the Implementation Guide and open the corresponding rendered FHIR artefact pages.

Conformance Requirements

The keywords SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are normative and indicate the degree of conformance required by this guide already defined in Conformace/General Requirements.

Foundational Resources

These Nigeria Core profiles provide the patient, workforce, organisation, location, relationship, and encounter context required by HIV programme artefacts.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG Patient Represents the person receiving HIV prevention, testing, care, treatment, laboratory, pharmacy, transfer, or follow-up services. Systems exchanging patient-level HIV data SHALL use this profile and populate all mandatory and Must Support elements relevant to the workflow.
NG Practitioner Identifies clinicians, counsellors, pharmacists, laboratory personnel, and other health workers participating in HIV services. Systems recording an individual health worker SHALL use this profile and preserve the practitioner's identifiers and professional details where available.
NG PractitionerRole Represents the role, specialty, organisation, and service context in which a practitioner delivers HIV care. Systems that exchange the practitioner's operational role SHALL use this profile and reference the associated Practitioner and Organization.
NG RelatedPerson Represents caregivers, treatment supporters, guardians, next of kin, and other persons related to an HIV client. Systems exchanging related-person information SHALL use this profile and SHALL limit disclosure to the authorised care or programme purpose.
NG Organization Identifies health facilities, laboratories, pharmacies, programme units, implementing partners, and other organisations involved in HIV services. Systems SHALL use this profile for participating organisations and SHALL use nationally governed identifiers where available.
NG Location Represents the physical or service-delivery location where HIV services, specimen collection, dispensing, or follow-up occur. Systems exchanging location information SHALL use this profile and reference the responsible Organization where applicable.
NG Encounter Provides the care-contact context for HIV assessment, counselling, treatment, laboratory, pharmacy, and follow-up activities. Clinical HIV resources associated with a visit SHOULD reference a conformant encounter, and systems SHALL preserve encounter status, class, period, patient, and service location.

HIV Clinical Resources

These Nigeria Core clinical profiles support the extraction, persistence, exchange, and reuse of structured data collected through HIV programme forms and workflows.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG Condition Represents HIV diagnosis, opportunistic infections, tuberculosis, comorbidities, pregnancy-related conditions, and other clinically relevant problems. Systems exchanging diagnoses or problem-list entries SHALL use this profile and apply the terminology binding defined for the coded condition.
NG Observation Represents HIV test results, viral load, CD4, adherence findings, screening results, clinical measurements, and other observed data. Systems exchanging observations SHALL use the applicable Nigeria Core Observation profile, preserve status and effective time, and use required codes and UCUM units where specified.
NG ServiceRequest Represents requests for laboratory tests, referrals, procedures, and other HIV-related services. Systems creating or receiving HIV service orders SHALL use this profile and populate the patient, requester, requested service, status, intent, and supporting context.
NG Specimen Represents specimens collected for viral load, EID, CD4, drug-resistance, chemistry, haematology, and other HIV-related testing. Laboratory workflows SHALL use this profile when specimen identity, type, collection, handling, or traceability is exchanged.
NG DiagnosticReport Groups and communicates authorised HIV laboratory or diagnostic results and their interpretations. Systems issuing diagnostic reports SHALL use this profile, reference the relevant ServiceRequest, Specimen, and Observation resources where available, and preserve report status.
NG MedicationRequest Represents prescribed ART, PrEP, PEP, TB preventive therapy, opportunistic-infection medicines, and related treatments. Prescribing systems SHALL use this profile, identify the patient and prescriber, and use the applicable medication terminology and dosage instructions.
NG MedicationStatement Represents medicines reported as currently or previously taken by the client, including prior ART and transferred-in regimens. Systems exchanging medication history SHALL use this profile and distinguish reported use from an active prescription or dispensing event.
NG AllergyIntolerance Represents allergies, intolerances, and clinically relevant adverse reactions affecting HIV treatment choices. Systems exchanging allergy information SHALL use this profile and preserve verification status, clinical status, substance, and reaction details where known.
NG Procedure Represents completed HIV-related procedures and interventions that are not adequately represented as observations or medication events. Systems claiming Procedure support SHALL use this profile and record patient, status, performed time, performer, and coded procedure where applicable.
NG Flag Represents actionable HIV care alerts, cautions, follow-up warnings, or programme attention markers. Systems using flags SHALL state the flag status, category, code, subject, and applicable period, and SHALL protect sensitive flag content from unauthorised disclosure.
NG Task Coordinates HIV referrals, specimen movement, follow-up, treatment actions, pharmacy fulfilment, and other workflow assignments. Workflow systems SHALL use this profile when exchanging actionable work and SHALL preserve task status, intent, owner, focus, and business identifiers.

Logical Models (Registers and Worksheets)

These logical models describe the data structures used for longitudinal programme monitoring, clinical follow-up, laboratory monitoring, client tracking, and pharmacy operations.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG HIV National ART Register Defines the longitudinal data structure for national ART enrolment, treatment, monitoring, outcomes, and programme follow-up. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG HIV Enhanced Adherence Counselling Monitoring Register Defines the monitoring structure for enhanced adherence counselling sessions and virologic-failure follow-up. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG HIV Integrated Laboratory Monitoring Register Defines the longitudinal laboratory monitoring structure for HIV tests, specimens, results, and viral-load disaggregation. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG HIV Tracking Register Defines the structure for tracing clients, recording contact attempts, outcomes, DSD information, and discontinuation or defaulting reasons. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG HIV Pharmacy Daily Worksheet Defines daily pharmacy activity, medicine movement, regimen and DSD summaries, and HEI prophylaxis information. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.

Implementation Guidance

Implementers should use the Questionnaire artefacts to create interoperable electronic HIV forms and corresponding QuestionnaireResponse resources to represent completed submissions. Where structured clinical reuse is required, QuestionnaireResponse content should be extracted or mapped to the applicable foundational and clinical profiles.

Logical models provide programme-oriented definitions for registers and worksheets and should be mapped to appropriate clinical, administrative, medication, laboratory, workflow, and reporting FHIR resources. Measure artefacts define computable indicator logic, while MeasureReport resources carry the aggregate results for a defined reporting period.

CodeSystems define authoritative programme codes. ValueSets identify the codes permitted for specific data elements and reporting disaggregations. ConceptMaps support translation between Nigeria HIV local concepts, LOINC, and SNOMED CT; mappings should be validated through programme and terminology governance before use in clinical decision-making.

Implementations SHALL preserve published canonical URLs, identifiers, terminology system URIs, code-system versions where supplied, references, cardinalities, invariants, and required bindings. HIV data and documents SHALL be exchanged only for authorised purposes and protected in accordance with applicable privacy, security, consent, audit, and data-minimisation requirements.
