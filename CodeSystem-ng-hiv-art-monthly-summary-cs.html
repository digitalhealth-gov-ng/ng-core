CodeSystem: NG HIV ART Monthly Summary Codes

Official URL: https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/CodeSystem/ng-hiv-art-monthly-summary-cs Version: 0.0.0
Active as of 2026-07-29 Computable Name: NgHivArtMonthlySummaryCS
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113884.3.9944.1.16.20

Indicator, stratifier-dimension and local disaggregation codes for the Nigeria ART Monthly Summary Form revised November 2025.

This Code system is referenced in the content logical definition of the following value sets:

  • NG HIV ART Monthly Age Groups
  • NG HIV ART Monthly AHD Categories
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Cervical Screening Age Groups
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Cervical Screening Results
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Cervical Screening Timing
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Cervical Treatment Types
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Cryptococcal Meningitis Results
  • NG HIV ART Monthly CrAg Age Groups
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Serum CrAg Results
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Summary Indicators
  • NG HIV ART Monthly Interruption Outcomes
  • NG HIV ART Monthly MMD Duration
  • NG HIV ART Monthly TB and AHD Age Groups

This case-sensitive code system https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/CodeSystem/ng-hiv-art-monthly-summary-cs defines the following codes:

CodeDisplayDefinition
ART1 ART1 (TX_NEW) Number of people living with HIV newly started on ART during the month, excluding ART transfer-in.
ART2 ART2 (TX_CURR) Number of people living with HIV currently receiving ART during the month.
ART3 ART3 (TX_PVLS_D) Number of people living with HIV on ART for at least six months with a viral-load test result within the last 12 months.
ART4 ART4 (TX_PVLS_N) Number of people living with HIV on ART for at least six months with virologic suppression of 1000 copies/mL or less within the last 12 months.
ART5 ART5 (TX_ATRR) Number of people living with HIV on ART who had treatment interruption during the reporting month, disaggregated by tracking outcome.
ART6 ART6 (TX_RTT) Number of people living with HIV who restarted ART during the month.
ART7 ART7 (TX_TO) Number of people living with HIV who transferred out during the month.
ART8 ART8 (TX_TI) Number of people living with HIV who transferred in during the month.
ART9 ART9 (ART_INSURANCE) Number of people living with HIV on ART with current health-insurance cover.
ART10 ART10 (TX_TB_SCRN) Number of people living with HIV on ART, including PMTCT clients, who were clinically screened for TB in HIV treatment settings.
ART11 ART11 (TX_TB_SCRN_POS) Number of people living with HIV on ART with presumptive TB during the month.
ART12 ART12 (TX_TB_TST) Number of people living with HIV on ART with presumptive TB who were tested for TB during the month.
ART13 ART13 (TX_TB_TST_POS) Number of people living with HIV on ART who had active TB disease.
ART14 ART14 (TX_TB) Number of people living with HIV on ART with active TB disease who initiated TB treatment.
ART15 ART15 (TB_PREV_D) Number of people living with HIV on ART who initiated TB preventive treatment.
ART16 ART16 (TB_PREV_N) Number of people living with HIV on ART who completed a course of TB preventive treatment among those who initiated it.
ART17 ART17 (TB_PREV_EVR) Number of people living with HIV currently on ART who had ever received TB preventive treatment.
ART18 ART18 (CARE_NEW) Number of people living with HIV newly enrolled in care during the month.
ART19 ART19 (AHD_SCRN) Number of people living with HIV with advanced HIV disease: WHO clinical stage 3 or 4, CD4 below 200 cells/mm3, or child aged five years or younger.
ART20 ART20 (AHD_SCRN_CRAG) Number of people living with HIV with advanced HIV disease screened for serum cryptococcal antigen.
ART21 ART21 (AHD_SCRN_CSF) Number of people living with HIV with advanced HIV disease and positive serum CrAg who were screened for cryptococcal meningitis by lumbar puncture.
ART22 ART22 (AHD_MEN_TX) Number of people living with HIV started on treatment for cryptococcal meningitis.
ART23 ART23 (CER_SCRN) Number of women living with HIV on ART screened for cervical cancer.
ART24 ART24 (CER_TX) Number of women living with HIV on ART who screened positive, were eligible for treatment, and received cryotherapy, thermal ablation or LEEP.
