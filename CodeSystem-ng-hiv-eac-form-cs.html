CodeSystem: NG HIV Enhanced Adherence Counselling Form Codes

Official URL: https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/CodeSystem/ng-hiv-eac-form-cs Version: 0.0.0
Active as of 2026-07-28 Computable Name: NgHivEacFormCS
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113884.3.9944.1.16.44

Local coded answers transcribed from the Nigeria Enhanced Adherence Counselling Form for adherence assessment, adherence barriers, service interventions and intervention tools.

This Code system is referenced in the content logical definition of the following value sets:

  • NG HIV EAC Adherence
  • NG HIV EAC Adherence Barriers
  • NG HIV EAC Intervention Services
  • NG HIV EAC Intervention Tools

This case-sensitive code system https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/CodeSystem/ng-hiv-eac-form-cs defines the following codes:

CodeDisplayDefinition
adherence-good Good The client demonstrates good adherence to the prescribed ART regimen.
adherence-fair Fair The client demonstrates fair adherence to the prescribed ART regimen.
adherence-poor Poor The client demonstrates poor adherence to the prescribed ART regimen.
barrier-forgot Forgot The client forgot to take one or more prescribed ART doses.
barrier-knowledge-beliefs Knowledge or beliefs Knowledge, understanding or beliefs affect the client's adherence.
barrier-side-effects Side effects Actual or anticipated medication side effects affect adherence.
barrier-physical-illness Physical illness Physical illness affects the client's ability or willingness to take ART.
barrier-substance-use Substance use Alcohol or other substance use affects adherence.
barrier-depression Depression Depression or depressive symptoms affect adherence.
barrier-pill-burden Pill burden The number, size or frequency of medicines affects adherence.
barrier-lost-ran-out-of-drugs Lost or ran out of drugs The client lost the medicines or exhausted the supply before the next refill.
barrier-transport Transport Transport availability or cost affects access to ART services or medicines.
barrier-child-refusing Child refusing A child refuses to take the prescribed ART medicines.
barrier-scheduling Scheduling The client's daily schedule or medication schedule affects adherence.
barrier-fear-of-disclosure Fear of disclosure Fear that the client's HIV status may be disclosed affects adherence.
barrier-family-partner Family or partner A family or partner-related issue affects adherence.
barrier-food-insecurity Food insecurity Lack of reliable access to adequate food affects adherence.
barrier-drug-stock-out Drug stock-out The medicine was unavailable because of a facility or supply-chain stock-out.
barrier-long-waiting-time Long waiting time Long waiting time at the service-delivery point affects adherence.
barrier-stigma Stigma Experienced or anticipated HIV-related stigma affects adherence.
barrier-other Other barrier An adherence barrier not represented by another code; specify separately.
service-education Education Education was provided to address adherence needs.
service-individual-counselling Individual counselling Individual adherence counselling was provided.
service-group-counselling Group counselling Group adherence counselling was provided.
service-peer-support Peer support Peer-based adherence support was provided.
service-treatment-buddy Treatment buddy A treatment buddy was established or supported.
service-extended-drug-pick-up Extended drug pick-up An extended medicine pick-up arrangement was provided.
service-community-art-group Community ART group The client was linked to or supported through a community ART group.
service-directly-observed-therapy Directly observed therapy Direct observation of treatment was provided or arranged.
service-other Other service intervention A service intervention not represented by another code; specify separately.
tool-pill-box Pill box A pill box was provided or recommended as an adherence aid.
tool-calendar Calendar A calendar was provided or recommended as an adherence aid.
tool-incentive-calendar-paediatric Incentive calendar (paediatric) A paediatric incentive calendar was provided or recommended.
tool-arv-swallowing-instruction ARV swallowing instruction Instruction on swallowing or administering ARV medicines was provided.
tool-written-instructions Written instructions Written medication or adherence instructions were provided.
tool-phone-call-sms Phone calls or SMS Phone calls or SMS messages were used or planned as adherence reminders.
tool-alarms Alarms An alarm or electronic reminder was used or recommended.
tool-other Other intervention tool An adherence tool not represented by another code; specify separately.
