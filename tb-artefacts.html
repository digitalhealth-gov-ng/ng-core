This page provides a consolidated catalogue of the FHIR artefacts developed for the Nigeria Tuberculosis (TB) programme. It brings together foundational and clinical profiles, structured programme forms, completed form examples, programme registers, laboratory and specimen workflows, referral and contact-management artefacts, and aggregate reporting artefacts. Links are relative to the Implementation Guide and open the corresponding rendered FHIR artefact pages.

Conformance Requirements

The keywords SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are normative and indicate the degree of conformance required by this guide already defined in Conformance/General Requirements.

Foundational Resources

These Nigeria Core profiles provide the patient, workforce, organisation, location, relationship, and encounter context required by TB programme artefacts.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG Patient Represents the person receiving TB screening, diagnosis, treatment, preventive therapy, laboratory, referral, transfer, contact investigation, or follow-up services. Systems exchanging patient-level TB data SHALL use this profile and populate all mandatory and Must Support elements relevant to the workflow.
NG Practitioner Identifies clinicians, TB programme officers, laboratory personnel, community workers, treatment supporters, and other health workers participating in TB services. Systems recording an individual health worker SHALL use this profile and preserve the practitioner's identifiers and professional details where available.
NG PractitionerRole Represents the role, specialty, organisation, and service context in which a practitioner delivers TB services. Systems that exchange the practitioner's operational role SHALL use this profile and reference the associated Practitioner and Organization.
NG RelatedPerson Represents treatment supporters, caregivers, household contacts, guardians, and other persons related to a TB client. Systems exchanging related-person information SHALL use this profile and SHALL limit disclosure to the authorised care or programme purpose.
NG Organization Identifies DOTS facilities, treatment centres, laboratories, referral sites, microscopy centres, community-linked providers, and other organisations involved in TB services. Systems SHALL use this profile for participating organisations and SHALL use nationally governed identifiers where available.
NG Location Represents the physical or service-delivery location where TB screening, treatment, specimen collection, laboratory, referral, or follow-up activities occur. Systems exchanging location information SHALL use this profile and reference the responsible Organization where applicable.
NG Encounter Provides the care-contact context for TB assessment, treatment, counselling, laboratory, preventive therapy, referral, transfer, and follow-up activities. Clinical TB resources associated with a visit SHOULD reference a conformant encounter, and systems SHALL preserve encounter status, class, period, patient, and service location.

TB Clinical Resources

These Nigeria Core clinical profiles support extraction, persistence, exchange, and reuse of structured data collected through TB programme forms, registers, laboratory processes, referral workflows, and treatment monitoring.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG Condition Represents confirmed or presumptive TB disease, drug-sensitive TB, drug-resistant TB, extrapulmonary TB, comorbidities, and other clinically relevant diagnoses. Systems exchanging TB diagnoses or problem-list entries SHALL use this profile and apply the terminology binding defined for the coded condition.
NG Observation Represents Xpert MTB/RIF, AFB smear, culture, HIV, weight, treatment monitoring, contact-screening findings, and other observed data. Systems exchanging observations SHALL use the applicable Nigeria Core Observation profile, preserve status and effective time, and use required codes and UCUM units where specified.
NG ServiceRequest Represents requests for Xpert MTB/RIF, AFB smear, culture, LPA, DST, other TB laboratory tests, referrals, and related services. Systems creating or receiving TB service orders SHALL use this profile and populate the patient, requester, requested service, status, intent, and supporting context.
NG Specimen Represents sputum and other specimens collected for Xpert MTB/RIF, AFB microscopy, culture, LPA, DST, TB-LAMP, LF-LAM, Quantiferon, and related testing. Laboratory and specimen-shipment workflows SHALL use this profile when specimen identity, type, collection, handling, transport, or traceability is exchanged.
NG DiagnosticReport Groups and communicates authorised TB laboratory and diagnostic results and interpretations. Systems issuing TB diagnostic reports SHALL use this profile, reference the relevant ServiceRequest, Specimen, and Observation resources where available, and preserve report status.
NG MedicationRequest Represents prescribed TB treatment, DR-TB regimens, TB preventive therapy, ART, CPT, and related medicines where relevant to TB care. Prescribing systems SHALL use this profile, identify the patient and prescriber, and use the applicable medication terminology and dosage instructions.
NG MedicationStatement Represents medicines reported as currently or previously taken by the client, including previous TB treatment, second-line drugs, ART, and preventive therapy. Systems exchanging medication history SHALL use this profile and distinguish reported use from an active prescription or dispensing event.
NG AllergyIntolerance Represents allergies, intolerances, and adverse reactions relevant to TB treatment choices. Systems exchanging allergy or intolerance information SHALL use this profile and preserve verification status, clinical status, substance, and reaction details where known.
NG Procedure Represents completed TB-related procedures and interventions not adequately represented as observations or medication events. Systems claiming Procedure support SHALL use this profile and record patient, status, performed time, performer, and coded procedure where applicable.
NG Task Coordinates TB referral, specimen movement, contact investigation, treatment interruption tracing, transfer, follow-up, and other workflow assignments. Workflow systems SHALL use this profile when exchanging actionable work and SHALL preserve task status, intent, owner, focus, and business identifiers.

Logical Models (Registers)

These logical models describe the programme-oriented structures used for TB registration, longitudinal treatment monitoring, presumptive-TB investigation, laboratory operations, specimen transport, community referral, and contact management.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG LGA/Health Facility Tuberculosis Central Register Defines the longitudinal central register for TB registration, treatment initiation, patient classification, bacteriological monitoring, treatment outcome, and TB/HIV activities. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG DR-TB Treatment Register Defines the longitudinal register for DR-TB registration, treatment regimen, baseline investigations, drug-susceptibility testing, monthly follow-up, TB/HIV activities, and treatment outcome. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG TB Presumptive Register Defines the register for presumptive DS-TB/DR-TB clients, referral source, specimen processing, investigations, HIV activities, investigation outcomes, and linkage to treatment. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG TB Laboratory Register Defines the TB laboratory register for specimen acceptance, patient and referral data, Xpert MTB/RIF, AFB, other tests, HIV status, result release, and reporting. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve its mandatory data semantics when mapping to operational FHIR resources; they SHALL document any omitted or transformed fields.
NG TB Specimen Shipment Register Defines specimen consignments from originating facilities to laboratories, including specimen entries, shipment officer, arrival, receiving personnel, temperature, and receipt remarks. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve shipment identity and specimen traceability when mapping to Specimen, Organization, PractitionerRole, Task, or related operational resources.
NG TB Community and Facility Referral Register Defines the site-level monthly register for client referral, receiving facility, TB confirmation, treatment initiation, and referral remarks. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve the referral cascade and receiving-facility outcome when mapping to operational FHIR resources.
NG TB Facility Contact Management Register Defines the index-patient-to-contact structure for screening, presumptive TB identification, diagnosis, treatment linkage, TPT eligibility, TPT initiation, monthly drug collection, and TPT outcome. Implementations using this programme tool SHOULD preserve the relationship between each index case and its contacts and SHALL document any transformation that flattens or separates this relationship.

Measure and MeasureReport (Aggregates)

These artefacts define aggregate TB reporting logic and support submission of programme reporting results.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG TB Quarterly DOTS and Microscopy Centre Measure Defines the aggregate reporting contract for the NTBLCP/TB 18 Quarterly Report on Newly Established DOTS and Microscopy Centres, including reporting population, newly established centres, cumulative centre counts, and population coverage measures. Aggregate reporting systems SHALL use the published Measure canonical and preserve the defined group and stratifier codes when producing corresponding MeasureReport instances.

Exchange Documents

These artefacts support document-based exchange and traceability of TB information between authorised facilities, laboratories, treatment centres, community programmes, repositories, and health information exchanges.

Artefact Purpose Conformance expectation
NG Documents Represents TB clinical documents, referral and transfer forms, discharge summaries, laboratory reports, specimen shipment documents, treatment records, and other document references exchanged between authorised systems. Systems exchanging TB documents SHALL use this profile, identify the patient and custodian, provide appropriate content metadata, and protect document confidentiality and integrity.
NG Provenance Records the origin, authorship, transformation, submission, and responsible agents for exchanged TB data and documents. Systems creating, transforming, or submitting TB exchange content SHOULD provide a conformant Provenance resource; regulated or signed exchanges SHALL include it where required by the workflow.

Implementation Guidance

Implementers should use the Questionnaire artefacts to create interoperable electronic TB programme forms and corresponding QuestionnaireResponse resources to represent completed submissions. QuestionnaireResponse items SHALL preserve the hierarchy, linkId, datatype, repetition, and conditional structure of the referenced Questionnaire. Where structured clinical reuse is required, response content should be extracted or mapped to the applicable foundational and clinical profiles.

Logical models provide programme-oriented definitions for national registers and should be mapped to appropriate clinical, administrative, laboratory, specimen, medication, workflow, and reporting FHIR resources. Fixed rows or month columns in source paper registers are represented as repeatable structures where appropriate so that the original programme semantics are retained without imposing paper-form limitations on electronic systems.

Specimen workflows SHOULD preserve traceability from specimen examination request and collection through shipment, laboratory receipt, processing, result release, and reporting. Referral and transfer workflows SHOULD preserve the relationship between the referring site, receiving facility, referral reason, acknowledgement or arrival, diagnostic outcome, treatment initiation, and continuity of care.

Contact-management implementations SHOULD preserve the relationship between the index TB patient and each contact, including screening, presumptive-TB identification, diagnostic testing, TB diagnosis, treatment linkage, TPT eligibility, preventive-therapy initiation, drug collection, and final outcome.

Measure artefacts define computable aggregate reporting contracts, while MeasureReport resources carry results for a defined reporting period. Implementations SHALL preserve the published Measure canonical, reporting period, group codes, population codes, stratifiers, scores, reporter information, and other required MeasureReport elements.

Implementations SHALL preserve published canonical URLs, identifiers, references, cardinalities, invariants, required bindings, and validation constraints. TB data and documents SHALL be exchanged only for authorised purposes and protected in accordance with applicable privacy, security, consent, audit, and data-minimisation requirements.
