ValueSet: NG HIV ART Monthly Summary Indicators

Official URL: https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/ValueSet/ng-hiv-art-monthly-indicator-vs Version: 0.0.0
Draft as of 2026-07-29 Computable Name: NgHivArtMonthlyIndicatorVS
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113884.3.9944.1.48.35

The 24 ART monthly summary indicators.

References

This value set is not used here; it may be used elsewhere (e.g. specifications and/or implementations that use this content)

Logical Definition (CLD)

  • Include these codes as defined in https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/CodeSystem/ng-hiv-art-monthly-summary-cs version 📦0.0.0
    CodeDisplayDefinition
    ART1ART1 (TX_NEW)Number of people living with HIV newly started on ART during the month, excluding ART transfer-in.
    ART2ART2 (TX_CURR)Number of people living with HIV currently receiving ART during the month.
    ART3ART3 (TX_PVLS_D)Number of people living with HIV on ART for at least six months with a viral-load test result within the last 12 months.
    ART4ART4 (TX_PVLS_N)Number of people living with HIV on ART for at least six months with virologic suppression of 1000 copies/mL or less within the last 12 months.
    ART5ART5 (TX_ATRR)Number of people living with HIV on ART who had treatment interruption during the reporting month, disaggregated by tracking outcome.
    ART6ART6 (TX_RTT)Number of people living with HIV who restarted ART during the month.
    ART7ART7 (TX_TO)Number of people living with HIV who transferred out during the month.
    ART8ART8 (TX_TI)Number of people living with HIV who transferred in during the month.
    ART9ART9 (ART_INSURANCE)Number of people living with HIV on ART with current health-insurance cover.
    ART10ART10 (TX_TB_SCRN)Number of people living with HIV on ART, including PMTCT clients, who were clinically screened for TB in HIV treatment settings.
    ART11ART11 (TX_TB_SCRN_POS)Number of people living with HIV on ART with presumptive TB during the month.
    ART12ART12 (TX_TB_TST)Number of people living with HIV on ART with presumptive TB who were tested for TB during the month.
    ART13ART13 (TX_TB_TST_POS)Number of people living with HIV on ART who had active TB disease.
    ART14ART14 (TX_TB)Number of people living with HIV on ART with active TB disease who initiated TB treatment.
    ART15ART15 (TB_PREV_D)Number of people living with HIV on ART who initiated TB preventive treatment.
    ART16ART16 (TB_PREV_N)Number of people living with HIV on ART who completed a course of TB preventive treatment among those who initiated it.
    ART17ART17 (TB_PREV_EVR)Number of people living with HIV currently on ART who had ever received TB preventive treatment.
    ART18ART18 (CARE_NEW)Number of people living with HIV newly enrolled in care during the month.
    ART19ART19 (AHD_SCRN)Number of people living with HIV with advanced HIV disease: WHO clinical stage 3 or 4, CD4 below 200 cells/mm3, or child aged five years or younger.
    ART20ART20 (AHD_SCRN_CRAG)Number of people living with HIV with advanced HIV disease screened for serum cryptococcal antigen.
    ART21ART21 (AHD_SCRN_CSF)Number of people living with HIV with advanced HIV disease and positive serum CrAg who were screened for cryptococcal meningitis by lumbar puncture.
    ART22ART22 (AHD_MEN_TX)Number of people living with HIV started on treatment for cryptococcal meningitis.
    ART23ART23 (CER_SCRN)Number of women living with HIV on ART screened for cervical cancer.
    ART24ART24 (CER_TX)Number of women living with HIV on ART who screened positive, were eligible for treatment, and received cryotherapy, thermal ablation or LEEP.
