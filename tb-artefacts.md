## TB FHIR Artefacts

This page provides a consolidated catalogue of the FHIR artefacts developed for the Nigeria Tuberculosis (TB) programme. It brings together foundational and clinical profiles, structured programme forms, completed form examples, programme registers, laboratory and specimen workflows, referral and contact-management artefacts, and aggregate reporting artefacts. Links are relative to the Implementation Guide and open the corresponding rendered FHIR artefact pages.

### Conformance Requirements

The keywords **SHALL**, **SHALL NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** are normative and indicate the degree of conformance required by this guide already defined in [Conformance/General Requirements](general.md).

### Foundational Resources

These Nigeria Core profiles provide the patient, workforce, organisation, location, relationship, and encounter context required by TB programme artefacts.

| | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [NG Patient](StructureDefinition-ng-patient.md) | Represents the person receiving TB screening, diagnosis, treatment, preventive therapy, laboratory, referral, transfer, contact investigation, or follow-up services. | Systems exchanging patient-level TB data**SHALL**use this profile and populate all mandatory and Must Support elements relevant to the workflow. |
| [NG Practitioner](StructureDefinition-ng-practitioner.md) | Identifies clinicians, TB programme officers, laboratory personnel, community workers, treatment supporters, and other health workers participating in TB services. | Systems recording an individual health worker**SHALL**use this profile and preserve the practitioner's identifiers and professional details where available. |
| [NG PractitionerRole](StructureDefinition-ng-practitioner-role.md) | Represents the role, specialty, organisation, and service context in which a practitioner delivers TB services. | Systems that exchange the practitioner's operational role**SHALL**use this profile and reference the associated Practitioner and Organization. |
| [NG RelatedPerson](StructureDefinition-ng-related-person.md) | Represents treatment supporters, caregivers, household contacts, guardians, and other persons related to a TB client. | Systems exchanging related-person information**SHALL**use this profile and**SHALL**limit disclosure to the authorised care or programme purpose. |
| [NG Organization](StructureDefinition-ng-organization.md) | Identifies DOTS facilities, treatment centres, laboratories, referral sites, microscopy centres, community-linked providers, and other organisations involved in TB services. | Systems**SHALL**use this profile for participating organisations and**SHALL**use nationally governed identifiers where available. |
| [NG Location](StructureDefinition-ng-location.md) | Represents the physical or service-delivery location where TB screening, treatment, specimen collection, laboratory, referral, or follow-up activities occur. | Systems exchanging location information**SHALL**use this profile and reference the responsible Organization where applicable. |
| [NG Encounter](StructureDefinition-ng-encounter.md) | Provides the care-contact context for TB assessment, treatment, counselling, laboratory, preventive therapy, referral, transfer, and follow-up activities. | Clinical TB resources associated with a visit**SHOULD**reference a conformant encounter, and systems**SHALL**preserve encounter status, class, period, patient, and service location. |

### Implementation Guidance

Implementers should use the Questionnaire artefacts to create interoperable electronic TB programme forms and corresponding QuestionnaireResponse resources to represent completed submissions. QuestionnaireResponse items **SHALL** preserve the hierarchy, `linkId`, datatype, repetition, and conditional structure of the referenced Questionnaire. Where structured clinical reuse is required, response content should be extracted or mapped to the applicable foundational and clinical profiles.

Logical models provide programme-oriented definitions for national registers and should be mapped to appropriate clinical, administrative, laboratory, specimen, medication, workflow, and reporting FHIR resources. Fixed rows or month columns in source paper registers are represented as repeatable structures where appropriate so that the original programme semantics are retained without imposing paper-form limitations on electronic systems.

Specimen workflows **SHOULD** preserve traceability from specimen examination request and collection through shipment, laboratory receipt, processing, result release, and reporting. Referral and transfer workflows **SHOULD** preserve the relationship between the referring site, receiving facility, referral reason, acknowledgement or arrival, diagnostic outcome, treatment initiation, and continuity of care.

Contact-management implementations **SHOULD** preserve the relationship between the index TB patient and each contact, including screening, presumptive-TB identification, diagnostic testing, TB diagnosis, treatment linkage, TPT eligibility, preventive-therapy initiation, drug collection, and final outcome.

Measure artefacts define computable aggregate reporting contracts, while MeasureReport resources carry results for a defined reporting period. Implementations **SHALL** preserve the published Measure canonical, reporting period, group codes, population codes, stratifiers, scores, reporter information, and other required MeasureReport elements.

Implementations **SHALL** preserve published canonical URLs, identifiers, references, cardinalities, invariants, required bindings, and validation constraints. TB data and documents **SHALL** be exchanged only for authorised purposes and protected in accordance with applicable privacy, security, consent, audit, and data-minimisation requirements.
