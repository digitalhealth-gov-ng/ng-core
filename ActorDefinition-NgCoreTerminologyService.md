## ActorDefinition: NG Terminology Service

A **terminology service** providing computational access to Nigeria Core CodeSystems, ValueSets, ConceptMaps, and supported external terminologies. 

 
Defines the role responsible for consistent representation, validation, lookup, expansion, and translation of coded information used by Nigeria Core implementations.
