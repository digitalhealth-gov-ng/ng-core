Defines the 24 aggregate indicators (ART1-ART24) and reporting dimensions used by the Nigeria National ART Monthly Summary Form revised November 2025. The Measure provides a stable FHIR reporting contract for facility-level monthly HIV programme reporting. The current population criteria are placeholders and do not yet constitute executable patient-level logic.
