ValueSet: NG HIV EAC Adherence Barriers

Official URL: https://fhir-ig.digitalhealth.gov.ng/ValueSet/ng-hiv-eac-barriers-vs Version: 0.0.0

Barriers to ART adherence recorded during Enhanced Adherence Counselling.

References

  • NG HIV Enhanced Adherence Counselling Form Questionnaire

    CodeDisplayDefinition
    barrier-forgotForgotThe client forgot to take one or more prescribed ART doses.
    barrier-knowledge-beliefsKnowledge or beliefsKnowledge, understanding or beliefs affect the client's adherence.
    barrier-side-effectsSide effectsActual or anticipated medication side effects affect adherence.
    barrier-physical-illnessPhysical illnessPhysical illness affects the client's ability or willingness to take ART.
    barrier-substance-useSubstance useAlcohol or other substance use affects adherence.
    barrier-depressionDepressionDepression or depressive symptoms affect adherence.
    barrier-pill-burdenPill burdenThe number, size or frequency of medicines affects adherence.
    barrier-lost-ran-out-of-drugsLost or ran out of drugsThe client lost the medicines or exhausted the supply before the next refill.
    barrier-transportTransportTransport availability or cost affects access to ART services or medicines.
    barrier-child-refusingChild refusingA child refuses to take the prescribed ART medicines.
    barrier-schedulingSchedulingThe client's daily schedule or medication schedule affects adherence.
    barrier-fear-of-disclosureFear of disclosureFear that the client's HIV status may be disclosed affects adherence.
    barrier-family-partnerFamily or partnerA family or partner-related issue affects adherence.
    barrier-food-insecurityFood insecurityLack of reliable access to adequate food affects adherence.
    barrier-drug-stock-outDrug stock-outThe medicine was unavailable because of a facility or supply-chain stock-out.
    barrier-long-waiting-timeLong waiting timeLong waiting time at the service-delivery point affects adherence.
    barrier-stigmaStigmaExperienced or anticipated HIV-related stigma affects adherence.
    barrier-otherOther barrierAn adherence barrier not represented by another code; specify separately.
